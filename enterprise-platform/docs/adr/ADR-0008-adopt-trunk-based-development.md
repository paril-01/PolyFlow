# ADR-0008: Adopt trunk-based development

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Short-lived branches reduce merge conflicts.

## Decision
We will adopt trunk-based development.

## Category
Process

## Author
David Kim (david.kim@ecp.dev)

## Date
2024-07-11

## Consequences
- Positive: Improves process capabilities
- Negative: Adds operational complexity
- Risks: Team needs training on new technology
