# Knowledge Transfer Protocol

## Trigger
Team member departure, ownership transition, new team member onboarding, or bus factor concern.

## Process
1. **Identify knowledge areas**: What does this person know that others don't?
2. **Document undocumented knowledge**: Code architecture, system behavior, operational procedures
3. **Conduct sessions**: Structured walkthroughs of key systems
4. **Update documentation**: Ensure all docs are current
5. **Shadow and pair**: New owner shadows current owner for a period
6. **Verify transfer**: New owner can operate independently
7. **Record transition**: Historian Agent records the knowledge transfer

## Knowledge Areas to Cover
- Architecture decisions and rationale
- Known issues and workarounds
- Operational procedures and runbooks
- Key contacts and dependencies
- Gotchas and tribal knowledge
