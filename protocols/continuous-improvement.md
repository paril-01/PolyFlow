# Continuous Improvement Protocol

## Trigger
End of sprint, end of project phase, after incidents, or quarterly review.

## Process
1. **Reflect**: What went well? What didn't? What surprised us?
2. **Gather data**: Metrics (velocity, defect rate, deployment frequency, incident count)
3. **Identify improvements**: Specific, actionable changes
4. **Prioritize**: Which improvements have the highest impact?
5. **Implement**: Treat improvements as engineering work (plan, execute, verify)
6. **Measure**: Did the improvement have the desired effect?
7. **Record**: Historian Agent records the retrospective and outcomes

## Improvement Categories
- **Process**: How we work (workflows, approvals, communication)
- **Technical**: How we build (tools, frameworks, patterns)
- **Quality**: How we ensure correctness (testing, reviews, standards)
- **Knowledge**: How we share and preserve knowledge (documentation, training)

## Key Metrics to Track
- Deployment frequency
- Lead time for changes
- Mean time to recovery (MTTR)
- Change failure rate
- Defect escape rate
- Technical debt trend
