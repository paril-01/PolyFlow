# Architecture Review Protocol

## Trigger
New architecture proposal, significant design change, or periodic architecture review.

## Process
1. **Gather artifacts**: Design docs, ADRs, diagrams
2. **Review against requirements**: Does the architecture meet all requirements?
3. **Review against principles**: SOLID, separation of concerns, loose coupling
4. **Evaluate scalability**: Will it handle growth?
5. **Evaluate reliability**: What are the failure modes?
6. **Evaluate security**: What is the attack surface?
7. **Evaluate cost**: What are the infrastructure implications?
8. **Check consistency**: Does it follow established patterns?
9. **Document findings**: Using [Review Report Template](../templates/review-report.md)
10. **Issue verdict**: Approve, Conditional, or Reject

## Participants
- Maker Agent (author)
- Reviewer Agent (reviewer)
- Relevant stakeholders
